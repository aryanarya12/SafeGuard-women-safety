About SafeGuard

SafeGuard is a comprehensive women safety network designed to empower women with real-time information about their surroundings and provide immediate assistance in times of need.

Our platform combines advanced technology including crime mapping, facial recognition, GPS tracking, and emergency alert systems to create a safer environment for women in urban areas.

Our Mission

To leverage technology and community cooperation to significantly reduce crimes against women and provide a secure, empowering environment for all.

  • Real-time crime data analysis
  • AI-powered threat detection
  • Intelligent route optimization
  • Instant emergency response
  • Community-driven safety network
  • Privacy-first design
